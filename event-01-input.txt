About Event
​Make your own frontier

​November 2 | San Francisco

​Fireworks, Microsoft, and NVIDIA are handing you one challenge: take open models and make your own frontier, on a task you define. Train a specialized model, build a smarter harness, route across models, whatever gets you there. Best result wins $10,000!

​The challenge

​Pick a task you care about and deploy a model that serves it in production. Build an eval using your own data, synthetic data, or a public set. Then measure the tradeoffs your deployment makes: quality vs. efficiency, accuracy vs. latency, pass rate vs. cost. Tune at least one dimension against your baseline, show the business value, and show how far you pushed it.

Think you can make it? Let's see it!

​Top prizes + additional

​1st place: $10,000

​2nd place: $5,000

​3rd place: $3,000

+NVIDIA DGX Spark, and others!

​Agenda (subject to change)

​9:00 AM Doors open

​10:30 AM Build begins

​4:30 PM Submissions close, judging begins, reception & networking

​7:00 PM Demos and awards

​Breakfast, lunch, snacks, and drinks served throughout.

Early bird gets the worm! Must have an approved registration and once we are at max capacity the doors will be closed.

Teams must have 2-5 members- single person teams will not be accepted. Every participant must register.

​For Microsoft's Data Privacy Notice please visit: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/data-privacy-notice#mainoverview
All registrations from this event will be shared with our co-hosts Microsoft and NVIDIA

Today’s the day! Fireworks: Deploy starts at 9 AM
​Good morning, builders!

​We’ll see you today at TERRA, 511 Harrison St, San Francisco for Fireworks: Deploy.

Check-in, breakfast and team formation run from 9–10 AM. Coming solo? We’ll help match you into a team of 2–4.

Bring your laptop, charger, task ideas and any public, synthetic or owned data you have permission to use.

Your challenge: use open models to beat a closed-model baseline on quality or efficiency. Come ready to define your task, build an evaluation set, and train a specialized model or shape a harness.

Today’s key times - PST
- 10:00 AM: Kickoff
- 10:30 AM: Building begins
- 12:30–1:30 PM: Lunch
- 4:30 PM sharp: Building ends and submissions close
- 4:30–7:00 PM: Judging and reception
- 7:00–7:30 PM: Top six finals and awards
- 8:00 PM: Event wraps up

Optional technical sessions and mentor support will be available during the day. Establish your baseline early and leave time to evaluate and submit!

Prizes: $10,000 for first · $5,000 for second · $3,000 for third. Plus additional prizes!

Let’s build something great.
